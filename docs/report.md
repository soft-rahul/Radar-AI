# ShelfRadar study: who do AI search engines recommend for whey protein in India?

_Run 2 · recorded 2026-10-06 · 100 SerpApi searches · generated by `scripts/write_report.py` from the saved answers_

## Setup
- **Questions:** 5 real buying questions found through Google autocomplete and People Also Ask (`study/questions.json`), each in English, Hindi and Hinglish.
- **Engines (all via SerpApi):** Google AI Overview (`google` + `google_ai_overview` follow-up), Google AI Mode (`google_ai_mode`), Bing Copilot (`bing_copilot`).
- **Where:** English from Mumbai, Hindi and Hinglish from Delhi (`location`, `gl=in`, `hl`). Copilot takes only the question text.
- **Repeats:** every cell asked 2 times with `no_cache=true`.
- **Answers:** 90 cells · 85 with an AI answer · 5 where Google showed none · 0 errors.
- **Brands tracked:** 27 (`study/whey.json`).
- **Statistics:** every share has a 95% Wilson range. A difference is reported as a finding only when the two ranges do not overlap; otherwise it is a tie.

## Findings

### 1. The same question rarely gets the same brand list twice
In **33 of 40** repeated pairs (same question, engine and language, asked again with no cache) the set of brands named changed: 82% (95% range 68%–91%). A one-shot "AI ranking" is not a stable number; visibility has to be measured as a probability. This matches SparkToro and Gumshoe's 2026 study, which found AI brand lists repeat less than 1% of the time.

### 6. Where AI Overview gets its sources
- 88% of 184 AI Overview sources are not among the top-10 organic results for the same search (by page; 72% by website). For comparison, Ahrefs (March 2026, US) found 38% of AI Overview citations in the top 10, i.e. 62% outside.
- Source types across all engines: review blogs and other sites 307 (53%), shops 133 (23%), brands' own websites 56 (10%), news media 45 (8%), Reddit/YouTube/Quora/Instagram 29 (5%), Google/Bing pages 7 (1%). Shops and review blogs are cited far more than brands' own websites.

## Accuracy of the brand counting
See [eval.md](eval.md): a human checked 30 answers (precision 100%, 0 false alarms); a coverage audit found brands missing from the list, which were added before this report was generated.

## Limits
- One category (whey protein), five questions, two repeats per cell: a pilot, not a census. Ranges are wide for smaller brands.
- One city per language; Copilot is not localised.
- AI answers change over time; this is a snapshot of the recording date.
- The stance reading is by an LLM; quotes are verified, but recommended vs neutral is a judgement. Brands added after the reading have no stance yet unless the fact-check was re-run.
- Mentions inside cited page titles do not count; only the AI's own words.
